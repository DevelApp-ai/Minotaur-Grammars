# test-grammars

Test fixtures consumed by the [ENFAStepLexer-StepParser](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/ENFAStepLexer-StepParser)
test suite, benchmarks, and demo (see issue #9). They are **not marketplace
content**: several are deliberately minimal or invalid to exercise error
paths, so they carry no per-grammar `minotaur-metadata.json`.

All fixtures are authored by DevelApp and MIT licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
